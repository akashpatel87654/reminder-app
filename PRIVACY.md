# SubTrack Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-09-30_

SubTrack helps you remember when your subscriptions renew or expire.

## What we store
- Your email address (to sign you in and send reminders).
- Your timezone and reminder preferences.
- The subscriptions you enter: name, price, currency, dates, reminder settings, and any optional URL, category or notes.

We never ask for or store card numbers, bank details, or passwords for other services.

## How we use it
- To show your subscriptions and spending totals in the app.
- To schedule reminder notifications on your device.
- To email you reminders, if email reminders are on. Every email has a one-click link to stop emails for that subscription, and you can turn emails off entirely in Settings.

## Who processes it
- Supabase — database and sign-in.
- Gmail — sending reminder and sign-in emails.

We don't sell your data, show ads, or use tracking or analytics SDKs.

## Your control
- Export everything as CSV from Settings.
- Delete your account from Settings. This permanently deletes your profile and all subscriptions.
