# Pingo Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-10-01_

Pingo is a subscription reminder app. It helps you keep track of your subscriptions and free trials and reminds you before they renew, expire or start charging.

## What we collect
- **Email address** — to sign you in with a one-time code and to send reminder emails.
- **Your subscriptions** — the details you type in: name, price, currency, type, billing cycle, purchase date, next renewal date, reminder days, colour, and any optional category, manage link or notes.
- **Preferences** — your timezone, reminder time, default reminder days, currency, and whether email reminders are on.

We never ask for or store card numbers, bank details, or passwords for other services. Pingo does not read your inbox, SMS or other apps.

## How we use it
- To show your subscriptions, upcoming renewals and spending totals in the app.
- To schedule reminder notifications on your device. These are created on your phone; no device identifier is sent to us.
- To email you reminders when email reminders are on. Every reminder email has a one-click link to stop emails for that subscription, and you can turn email reminders off in Settings.

## Who processes it
- **Supabase** — stores your account and subscriptions (encrypted in transit).
- **Google (Gmail)** — delivers sign-in and reminder emails.

We don't sell or share your data with anyone else, show ads, or use tracking or analytics SDKs.

## Your controls
- **Export** everything as CSV from Settings.
- **Delete your account** in the app: Settings → *delete account*. This permanently deletes your profile, subscriptions and reminder history straight away.

## Deleting your account without the app
If you can't use the app, email **heatmonks.venture@gmail.com** from the address you signed up with, subject "Delete my Pingo account". We delete your account and all associated data within 7 days and confirm by email. Nothing is kept after deletion.

## Children
Pingo is not directed at children under 13 and is intended for users 18 and over.

## Changes
If this policy changes, we'll update the date above.
